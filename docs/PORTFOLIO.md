Developed MedRec CareFlow, a Flask healthcare workflow prototype integrating encrypted clinical records, role-based access, audited clinician review, a reproducible ETL/ML pipeline, and automated tests and container delivery.

Use this wording only after personally reviewing and understanding the implementation. Do not claim production use, clinical validation, privacy-law certification, or measured patient outcomes.

## Three-minute demo

1. Open the patient overview and explain the check-in / review / summary loop.
2. Submit a fictional symptom check-in and show its pending state.
3. Sign in as a clinician. Explain the experimental suggestion and independently enter an assessment and notes.
4. Return to the patient view and download the care summary.
5. Show the ownership test and encrypted database test.
6. Explain the duplicate-data finding and why a perfect score is not clinical evidence.
7. Show the GitHub Actions workflow and model card.
